On 7th and 8th October 2025, me and two friends attended the Petaling Jaya Innovation Conference on Education (PUICE). It is an annual conference hosted by the district's education office for students and educators from countless schools to present and compete with one another on various categories. This year's PUICE is held at SJKC Puay Chai 2's hall, which is nearby my school.

To be honest, the conference feels like its longer than it should be. We were there from 8 am to 4 pm for two days straight, with most of the mornings filled with slots for teachers to present and showcase their research and innovations. Additionally, the hall was overcrowded. Each booth is provided two tables, three chairs, and there were hundreds of booths. Thank god nobody amongst us felt claustrophobic in the hall.
