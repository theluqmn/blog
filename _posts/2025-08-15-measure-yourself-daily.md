months ago, i attended a lecture at school about managing stress and got a handy notebook. i decided to turn that notebook to my life "organiser", where i keep track of my daily objectives and happenings - and by the end of this post, you will be convinced on why you should do it too.

<!--more-->

## why bother?

i just find it pleasing to be able to visually see my performance and objectives at a glimpse. i have tried using various different planners before, digitally and physically, but i found out that none is as flexible or customisable as making one myself on a notebook using some pen and pencil.

## the notebook

the notebook is merely a simple A5 notebook with writing lines. that's it, lots of flexibility, and just enough things to get started. i started looking up for layouts and designs on pinterest and youtube, on how i should design each page (one page = one week). it changes from time to time, so the design isnt consistent front-back.

## what to track

- — **notes** - important things to keep in mind.
- — **main targets** - your main targets for the week (eg: min. 2hrs of studying daily)
- — **objectives** - the objectives for the week, each has a due day (eg: MON, TUE, FRI).
- — **daily tracker** - one for each day of the week.
  - — highlights/notes for the day
  - — a brief summary of the day
  - — habit tracker
  - — data boxes
- — **summary** - summarise the week.

below everything, i have a little **self-reward** section where i add what my self-reward is for completing all the objectives, or when i finish the day. this helps by giving a slight boost of morale to keep going.

### habit tracker

i make little circles in a line for each day, each circle corresponding to a specific habit (eg: waking up before 04:00, recap lessons before bed, etc.). this allows me to keep track of what habits i am able to consistently.

### data boxes

for things i want to track that can be measured numerically, i have some small boxes.

the things i track:

- — happiness^
- — stress^
- — productivity^
- — pushups
- — sleep'
- — study'
- — github commits
- — balance (wallet balance)

^ = measured from 0 to 100, ' = measured in hours.

this allows me to have a good glimpse of how well im performing everyday.

## what my a week of my notebook looks like

the layout and design of each page is up to your needs and creativity! every week does not have to look the same, and you can always change or modify the design according to your current needs and aesthetics. dont want something? you can get rid of it. you think something is worth tracking and writing about? feel free to add it to the layout!
