last weekend, i started a new project titled [kssm-sk](https://theluqmn.com/kssm-sk) to help out my classmate who is changing her elective subject from biology to computer science. kssm-sk consists of various resources, as well as solutions to textbook activities and questions.

<!--more-->

## how it started

the SK (sains komputer/computer science) teacher only gave her ~6 days to study the entire chapter 1 (which by the way, consists of close to 200 pages), hence i offered to help her out. i lended her my notes, handouts and exercises, and even went as far as studying SK with her. SK in SMK schools are taught in malay, therefore kssm-sk is written almost entirely in malay.

most of the coding in SK is written with java, a language that i originally dislike - but in pursuit of teaching her, started being okay with (still not liking it, but at least i dont dislike it anymore).

## what kssm-sk has

kssm-sk contains simplified notes for quick revisions (click here: [nota ringkas](https://theluqmn.com/kssm-sk/tingkatan_4/nota_ringkas)), solutions to textbook activities (coding ones only), and my own personal answers to questions and challenges found in the textbook. all the code can be found on the [kssm-sk github repository](https://github.com/theluqmn/kssm-sk).

### other resources

though at the moment there are no other resources available, i do have plans to include PDFs and videos related to SK soon!

## how it works

the website which is default GitHub Pages themed is hosted on GitHub Pages with its code stored inside the /docs folder, whilst all the activities, code and etc. are kept inside folders according to the grade (tingkatan 4 or 5). the code is not shown on the website, so you would need to read it directly from GitHub.
